# Asset provenance

The supplied AFCON FINISHES logo was cropped to its artwork bounds, keeping the original pixel geometry, letterforms, spacing and aspect ratio. The white background was converted to transparency and the lettering recoloured ivory/charcoal for contrast. It was not redrawn or recreated with a font.

Clean photographic areas of the supplied approved AI concept were extracted as separate WebP assets for the three finish swatches, residential living room and restaurant. No screenshot, screenshot text or composite page is used as the website. The smaller inspiration crops reflect the source concept's resolution; replace them with full-resolution photos as your portfolio develops.

The stucco-detail asset is a clean plaster-only crop of the contact background.

The hero and contact background were generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this website. These assets are concept imagery. Visible imagery disclosures were removed at the owner’s request.
